\noindent\textbf{Evidence fidelity (v6--v8).} Current-source citation binding, structured probe observations, and symmetric scanner-negative gates establish whether the evidence can support an actionable assessment. \noindent\textbf{Boundary authorization (v9--v10).} Static invariant-gap and differential guard-bypass routes in v9 provide alternatives for supporting a diagnosis, yet redundant consumer-read requirements still reject positive actions. Recognizing preloaded callers and intrinsic input contracts in v10 removes that mismatch between already available evidence and edit authorization. RSA and LangChain then convert in C3, illustrating why a public validator and an HTML producer require different boundary checks.

\noindent\textbf{Context and lifecycle completeness (v11--v12).} Completeness cues in v11 improve the review questions, but two C3 investigations abort during context packing after localizing their targets. v12 deduplicates protocol state, reserves context headroom, tracks execution within class spans, and clarifies entity, form-option, and validation-lifecycle contracts. Wagtail Forms supplies the third C3 repair, and all twenty qualification cells avoid repair errors. Across the matched progression, C3 citation rejections fall from 23 to one, total rejections change from 48 to 43, and turn-limit endings rise from two to six. The phases therefore identify distinct evidence, authorization, and execution requirements. They do not imply that every revision improves every efficiency metric. The recorded artifacts support comparison with the larger campaign.

The nested funnel in Table~\ref{tab:engineering}, Panel B, separates four observable stages. S1 records a hypothesis on a reference symbol, and S2 records a supported assessment on such a symbol. S3 requires a reference-file edit within the supported group, while S4 requires a nonempty retained patch within S3. The final columns count verified SecPass and new conversions inside S4, so they describe that retained subset rather than the campaign total. In the full DeepSeek C3 cohort, 252/387 cells reach S1, 97 reach S2, 89 reach S3, and 60 reach S4. Ten of these retained candidates achieve SecPass, including six conversions. Gemini C3 reaches 235/23/21/17 cells across S1--S4, with five retained security passes and four conversions, concentrating its largest loss between localization and support.

The earlier 1,712-cell audit provides a detailed explanation of localization-to-support losses on its frozen diagnostic population. DeepSeek C3 has 72 localized-without-supported cells and Gemini C3 has 129. Ten and five of those cells attempt a positive assessment or edit that the protocol rejects. Accepted refutations occur in 48 and 98, while inconclusive assessments occur in 56 and 25. These indicators can overlap within a cell. DeepSeek reaches the turn limit in forty gap cells, compared with three for Gemini, while repair errors or stops affect three and fourteen. Seventeen and twenty-three gap cells already have verified secure baselines, making the target's location insufficient to identify a missed repair. On verified FuncPass/SecFail starts, refutations occur in 34/53 DeepSeek and 75/100 Gemini gap cells. Together, these observations identify unsupported or overgeneralized diagnoses as a substantial bottleneck beyond the smaller set of explicitly rejected positive actions.

\subsection{Patch Retention and Counterfactual Rescue}
The historical 1,712-cell inventory contains 405 cells that write a patch and 141 nonempty buffers discarded to no-ops at finalization. Exact-current-patch attribution identifies seventy eligible cases whose discard is attributable to missing validation capacity or ceremony. Of these, 37 exhaust the two public-functional slots, 31 reach the turn limit before validating the current edit, and two miss a required post-edit probe rerun. A separate local recovery evaluates these saved candidates without regenerating them or overwriting the original outcomes. Thirty-nine pass public functionality, eighteen fail it, and thirteen remain execution-unresolved. Nine pass security, comprising eight preserved successes and one new Piccolo conversion in DeepSeek C1. Django Sessions C3, whose late edit motivated the audit, fails the public \code{test_invalid_key} assertion and therefore does not proceed to security evaluation. These results motivate exact-patch validation reuse and a final public-validation opportunity while retaining the distinction between written work and a verified repair. The archived buffers and rejection reasons make that engineering loss measurable even when the rescued candidate ultimately remains incorrect.

\subsection{Operational Errors as a Separate Failure Class}
The execution record also separates infrastructure outcomes from diagnosis and repair decisions. Across the complete Gemini campaign, 205 cells end with a recorded repair error. Of these, 153 contain the provider response \code{blockReason: OTHER}, 45 contain a \code{JSONDecodeError}, six contain an HTTP 500 error, and one contains another value error. The provider label is preserved exactly rather than assigned an inferred cause. DeepSeek records 107 repair errors. These comprise 63 explicit stop interruptions, 32 HTTP 429 responses, nine request deadlines, two value errors, and one connection error. Bounded retries apply to rejected or interrupted transport requests while retaining reservations for unresolved billing. Completed model responses are never silently rerolled. A repair error can still leave a final candidate or no-op with a complete official evaluation, which remains part of the matched outcome analysis. Keeping these records separate makes it possible to assess workflow reliability without attributing every unsuccessful cell to the investigator's security reasoning.

\rqfinding{RQ4 Finding}{The dominant repair losses span several stages, including unsupported diagnoses, exhausted validation opportunities, incomplete semantic fixes, and provider execution failures. Stage-linked actions, patches, and test outcomes identify the intervention each failure requires.}
