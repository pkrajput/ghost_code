\subsection{Iterative Engineering Progression (v6--v12)}
To understand how protocol design affects repair losses (RQ4), we trace seven iterative development revisions of our workflow (\texttt{v6} through \texttt{v12}, where \texttt{v12} is the final released configuration evaluated in Section~\ref{sec:results}) across a fixed ten-case diagnostic cohort (Table~\ref{tab:engineering}, Panel~A). It contains nine initially insecure outputs and one secure Wagtail control. Both configurations retain ten functional passes throughout. Final C3 preserves the control and repairs three cases, compared with one repair for C0.

\noindent\textbf{Evidence fidelity (v6--v8).} Current-source citation binding, structured probe observations, and symmetric scanner-negative gates make assessments auditable. Preserving GumTree parent-subtree operations and normalizing fragments restores missing guard descriptions, but does not resolve Wagtail's downstream rendering contract.

\noindent\textbf{Boundary authorization (v9--v10).} Static invariant-gap and differential guard-bypass routes support diagnoses. Recognizing preloaded callers and intrinsic input contracts removes redundant review requirements. RSA and LangChain then convert in C3.

\noindent\textbf{Context and lifecycle completeness (v11--v12).} Deduplicating state and reserving context headroom prevents two previously observed packing aborts. Class-span reachability and lifecycle cues support the third C3 repair, in Wagtail Forms. All twenty final qualification cells avoid repair errors, although C3 turn-limit endings rise from two to six across the progression.

\subsection{Stage-Level Investigation Funnel and Validation Scheduling}
Table~\ref{tab:engineering}, Panel B, follows nested stages. S1 records a hypothesis on a reference symbol, S2 a supported assessment there, S3 a reference-file edit within that supported group, and S4 a nonempty retained patch. DeepSeek C3 reaches 252/97/89/60 cells across S1--S4, with ten retained SecPass outcomes including six conversions. Gemini C3 reaches 235/23/21/17, with five passes and four conversions. These retained-subset counts differ from whole-campaign totals. The largest loss is between localization and support. Diagnostic trace review finds refutations, inconclusive assessments, overgeneralized hypotheses, and turn exhaustion alongside rejected positive actions. A reference location alone does not establish a defect, including on initially secure outputs.

A separate development recovery check evaluates seventy saved candidates discarded for exhausted functional-validation slots (37), reaching the turn limit before validation (31), or missing a post-edit probe rerun (two). Without regenerating patches, 39 pass public functionality, eighteen fail, and thirteen remain unresolved. Nine pass security, comprising eight preserved successes and one conversion. This motivates reserving final public-validation capacity and reusing validation only for an identical patch. Recovery records remain separate from original outcomes.

We compare retained diffs, reference patches, and official failing assertions on fifteen tasks outside the diagnostic cohort, fourteen from DeepSeek and one from Gemini, with functional success before and after editing (Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy}). Four patches address the wrong property, nine identify the right vulnerability class but leave an incomplete fix, and two violate the required failure behavior. Examples include lexical path normalization that misses symlink escape, validation that omits inherited connection fields, and a correct rejection using the wrong exception type. Symbol localization and plausible edits therefore need full input, lifecycle, and error-contract checks.

Operational errors remain separately observable. Gemini records 205 repair errors, comprising 153 \code{blockReason: OTHER} responses, 45 JSON decoding errors, six HTTP 500 errors, and one other value error. DeepSeek records 107, comprising 63 stop interruptions, 32 HTTP 429 responses, nine deadlines, two value errors, and one connection error. Provider labels are not assigned inferred causes. Bounded transport retries retain billing reservations, and completed responses are not regenerated. An errored cell can still have an officially evaluated retained candidate or no-op in the matched analysis.

\rqfinding{RQ4 Finding}{Losses arise during diagnosis, validation scheduling, semantic repair, and provider execution. Linking actions, patches, and terminal test outcomes distinguishes the engineering intervention each failure needs.}
